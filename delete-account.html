Delete your VAPOGO account

Applies to the apps VAPOGO (customers) and VAPOGO Partner (bus operators), both from Schodcat Tech.

Delete it inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and log in.
  2. Customer app: go to the Account tab and tap Delete account.
    Partner app: open the side menu and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Cannot open the app? Ask us to delete it

Email support@schodcattech.com from the email address of the account, with the subject "Delete my VAPOGO account", and tell us the mobile number on the account. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, mobile number, Google sign-in link and notification tokens.
  • Your trip requests, bookings, ratings and reviews.
  • For operators: all your buses, bus photos, driver details and the bookings on those buses.

What we keep, and why

  • A record that you accepted our Terms & Disclaimer (name, email, mobile number, version, time), as proof of what was agreed.
  • If your account was suspended, or a bus was taken down, after a report: the email, mobile number and bus registration number, so that the suspended account or bus cannot return under a new account.
  • Technical server logs for a limited time, for security.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. See our Privacy Policy for more.

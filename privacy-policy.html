VAPOGO Privacy Policy

Applies to the apps VAPOGO (customers, package in.vapogo.app) and VAPOGO Partner (bus operators, package in.vapogo.partner), and to the VAPOGO administration website.
Effective date: 14 October 2026.

VAPOGO is a marketplace where customers in India request to rent a whole tourist bus and bus operators answer those requests. VAPOGO does not take payments. The operator calls the customer and the two settle everything directly. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what choices you have.

Who is responsible for your data: Schodcat Tech, operating VAPOGO ("VAPOGO", "we", "us"). Contact: support@schodcattech.com.

1. What we collect

When you use the customer app

  • Account details: name, email address and mobile number. If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your Google name and verified email address from Google.
  • Trip requests: the bus you ask for, trip dates, pickup time, pickup and drop locations, number of passengers, purpose of the trip and any notes you type.
  • Location: if you tap "Near me" or "Use my current location", the app reads your phone's precise location while you are using the app. "Near me" sends your coordinates to our server only to sort buses by distance; we do not store them. "Use my current location" turns your position into an address (using OpenStreetMap, see section 4) and puts it in the pickup box, where you can edit it. The address you submit is saved with your request. We never track your location in the background.
  • Ratings, reviews and reports you write about a trip, an operator or a bus.
  • Notification token: a technical identifier of your phone so we can send you push notifications about your requests.
  • Advertising data: the customer app shows banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and information about how you interact with ads (see section 4).

When you use the Partner app (operators)

  • Account details: name, company name, email, mobile number, state, district and pincode (and your Google name and email if you use Google sign-in).
  • Bus details: registration number, vehicle category, seats, features, photos, base location (including coordinates if you set the bus location) and legal document details such as RC, tourist permit, insurance, road tax and pollution certificate numbers and expiry dates. If you ask for the "Verified" tick you email copies of your documents to us; we use them only to verify the bus.
  • Driver details you enter (driver name and mobile number). Please make sure the driver agrees to this. A driver's name and number are shared with a customer only if you choose to share them when you confirm a booking.
  • Booking activity: your answers to requests, cancellation reasons, trip outcomes, and any report you make about a customer.
  • Notification token (as above) and location when you ask the app to tag where a bus is based.

For everyone

  • Terms acceptance record: when you accept our Terms & Disclaimer we keep your name, email, mobile number, role, the version you accepted, the time (server time and your phone's time) and the app version. We keep this as proof of acceptance.
  • Technical data: our server logs include your IP address, the time and the request made. We use them for security, abuse prevention (for example rate limiting) and fixing faults.

3. Who sees your data inside VAPOGO

  • The operator of a bus you request sees your name, mobile number, email address, trip details and notes. The apps show contact details as Call / WhatsApp / Email links rather than printing them.
  • A customer sees the operator's company name and bus details, and, once the operator confirms the booking, the operator's mobile number (and a driver's name and number if the operator chooses to share them).
  • Reports are not shown to the person reported. The operator does not see which customer reported them, and a customer does not see which operator reported them.
  • VAPOGO administrators can see account, booking, bus and report details so that they can run and protect the service.

5. How long we keep data

  • Your account and trip history are kept until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, bookings, reviews and (for operators) buses, photos and drivers are deleted.
  • Kept after deletion: the terms acceptance record (proof of what you agreed to and when), and, if an account was suspended, or a bus was taken down, after a report, the email, mobile number and bus registration number, so that a suspended account or bus cannot simply return under a new account. Server logs are kept for a limited time.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your details in the app (Account in the customer app, My Profile in the Partner app).
  • Delete your account and data inside the app, or ask us to do it: see how to delete your account.
  • Location: you can refuse or withdraw the location permission in your phone's settings. The apps still work; you type the pickup address instead.
  • Notifications: you can switch them off in your phone's settings.
  • Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone's Google settings.
  • You may ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to erase it, and you may withdraw consent or complain, by emailing support@schodcattech.com. We follow the rights given by Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, login tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage, access to our systems is limited and protected, and we limit login attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your login details private.

8. Children

VAPOGO is meant for adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
